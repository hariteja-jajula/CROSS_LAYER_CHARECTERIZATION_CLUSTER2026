--- PAPER PARAGRAPH ---
To assess whether cross-layer features support predictive intervention,
we trained Random Forest and Gradient Boosting models to predict a job's
cross-layer tier from the submitting user's prior job history. Using a
temporal train/test split (January–August for training, September–December
for evaluation), the models achieve 70.4% accuracy on 8-class tier
prediction (1.3x over majority baseline) and 0.893 ROC AUC
on binary waste prediction. Feature ablation confirms that cross-layer
features (GPU utilization history, I/O patterns, tier distribution)
substantially improve predictive power over scheduler metadata alone,
quantifying the operational value of cross-layer telemetry correlation
for proactive resource management.
