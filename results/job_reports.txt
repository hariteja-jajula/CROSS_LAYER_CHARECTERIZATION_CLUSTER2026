we implement an automated per-job diagnostic report generator that
translates cross-layer classification into plain-English feedback
with specific, actionable recommendations. Applied retrospectively
to the full Polaris dataset, the system would generate actionable
reports for 142,397 waste-tier jobs (54.2% of submissions),

jobs with zero I/O, and 1,384 cb\_nodes tuning recommendations

diagnosis, and recommendation are fully automated from the three
telemetry sources, enabling a closed-loop operational workflow from
job completion to user notification.
